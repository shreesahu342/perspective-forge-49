# The Mirror of Perspective

### An AI Debate & Philosophy Roleplay Game

**The Mirror of Perspective (TMOP)** is an interactive philosophy-based game designed and developed by **Sarthika Sahu**.

The project explores philosophy not as something to simply read about, but as something to actively engage with through debate, roleplay, argumentation, and self-reflection.

---

## 🪞 What is The Mirror?

The Mirror is a game where players can enter philosophical debates with thinkers from different eras and perspectives.

Instead of simply learning what a philosopher believed, the player can **challenge their ideas, construct arguments, and experience philosophical disagreements interactively.**

The game is divided into multiple modes.

---

# ⚔️ Mode 1 — Debate

In Mode 1, players choose a philosopher from a range of historical and intellectual eras and enter an interactive debate with them.

### Choose Your Era

The game currently includes **39 philosophical archetypes** spanning different periods and traditions, including:

- Ancient Core Archetypes
- Classical Indian Thinkers
- Medievals
- Synthesis
- Bhakti Movement
- Early Modern
- Modern Conflict
- 19th Century Pressure
- 20th Century Breakdown
- Modern Indian Thinkers
- Contemporary Power & Language

Players can select a philosopher such as **Socrates or Plato**, choose their starting position, and begin a debate.

### 🧠 The Crack System

Arguments are evaluated according to their logical strength and correctness.

Successful arguments allow players to earn **Cracks**.

Cracks can then be used to unlock additional philosophers, creating a progression system based on philosophical engagement.

### 🔨 Forge Your Own Philosopher

Players can also create and **forge their own philosopher**, allowing them to construct an original philosophical perspective rather than only interacting with historical thinkers.

---

# 🎭 Mode 2 — Theater of Voices

The second mode, **Theater of Voices**, approaches philosophy through roleplay.

Instead of debating historical philosophers, the player can cast themselves and their **internal interlocutor** into different archetypal relationships.

These include:

- Parent & Child
- Prophet & Skeptic
- Giant & Citizen
- Relationship & Ship

The player roleplays through these contrasting voices and perspectives, exploring internal conflict, belief, doubt, power, and relationships.

Players earn **points** through this mode.

These points can then be used in **Mode 1 to purchase and unlock philosophers**, connecting the two experiences together.

---

# 🎯 Core Idea

The Mirror of Perspective is built around one central idea:

> **Philosophy becomes different when you stop reading arguments and start arguing back.**

The game turns philosophical thought into an interactive experience where the player is not merely an observer, but a participant.
